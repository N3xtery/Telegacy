Profiles

You can see your own profile by choosing the "View profile" option in the Profile menu, or the profile of a chat by choosing the same option in the Chat menu. The following information is displayed there: profile picture, name, handle, birthday and bio. You can change your data or data of a group or channel you own by editing the fields. To change the profile picture, click on it and choose an image you want. If you want to not show the year of your birthday, choose a date of the current year.
