Downloading files

The files in chats are shown as links. Click on one to start downloading the file you need. The files are downloaded in chunks of 1 MB each, and you can see the progress on the status bar. In case a file is located on a different datacenter from yours and you haven't downloaded anything from it before, you'll have to wait through the process of creating an authorization key for it. You can also download the full sizes of images by clicking on their right halves.
